For Gift Sake · Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026

In short: Your ledger (people, gifts, ideas, notes and photos) stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account. The developer has no server and never sees it. The free version shows a small ad bar provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device and usage information to show and measure ads, as described below. Buying “Remove ads” turns the ads off completely.

What the app stores

The people, gifts, occasions, ideas, budgets, photos, notes and your note signature are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, this data syncs through your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit) so it appears on your other devices. The developer cannot access it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you have bought “Remove ads”, For Gift Sake shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google’s software in the app may collect information such as your device type and settings, an app-specific device identifier, your approximate location derived from your IP address, crash and performance data, and how you interact with the ads. For Gift Sake does not ask for permission to track you across other companies’ apps and websites, so your device’s advertising identifier is not used. Nothing you write in your ledger is ever given to Google or used for ads.

Where the law requires it (for example in the UK and the European Economic Area), the app asks for your consent with Google’s consent form before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. Google’s use of this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Gift suggestions

On devices with Apple Intelligence, “Suggest gift ideas” uses Apple’s on-device model with what you have noted about that person. It runs entirely on your device; nothing is sent to the developer or to any other company.

Contacts

Only if you choose to import birthdays, For Gift Sake reads your contacts’ names and birthdays on your device so you can pick who to add. Only the people you select are saved. Contacts are never sent anywhere except your own iCloud sync.

Photos

Photos are chosen with the system photo picker, which gives the app only the images you pick. They are stored with your ledger.

Links you save

When you save a link as an idea, the app may load that web page to read its title and preview image. That request goes directly to the website you chose; nothing about you is added to it.

Widgets and the share extension

To show your next occasion and waiting thank-yous, the app writes a small summary to storage shared only with its own widgets and share extension on your device.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves it.

Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you share it yourself, for example an idea list, a thank-you note or a CSV export through the share sheet.

Purchases

“Remove ads” is a one-time in-app purchase through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and does not give the developer your payment details; the app only checks with the App Store whether the purchase belongs to your Apple Account.

Children

For Gift Sake is not directed at children under 13 and the developer does not knowingly collect information from them. Ad requests are handled by Google as described above.

Deleting your data

Delete items in the app, or delete the app to remove data from the device. To remove synced data, open Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage and delete For Gift Sake’s data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
