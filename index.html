Every gift, remembered

Gift Ledger tracks the gifts you give and receive, saves ideas for the people you love, and helps you send every thank-you note on time.

What Gift Ledger does

Thank-you notes

Every gift you receive lands on your thank-you list. Tap one to draft a note in a warm, short or formal tone, then move it from To write to Written to Sent.

Don’t-repeat warnings

Each person keeps a gift history. When you log a new gift, Gift Ledger warns you if you’ve given them something similar before.

Upcoming occasions

Import birthdays from Contacts (only the people you pick) and add holidays, anniversaries and events. Optional reminders arrive a week before.

Likes, sizes and suggestions

Note what each person loves, what to avoid and their sizes. On devices with Apple Intelligence, “Suggest gift ideas” proposes fresh ideas that avoid anything you’ve given before, privately on your device.

Shopping list

Tap the cart on the Ideas tab to see every planned gift: what still needs buying, what needs wrapping and what’s ready to give.

Ideas, budgets, events and milestones

Save ideas from any app’s share sheet, set season budgets, log a wedding or baby shower in seconds with event mode, and earn milestone medals along the way.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Your ledger lives on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account.

How do I sync between iPhone and iPad?

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on for Gift Ledger on both devices. Changes sync automatically through your private iCloud database. The first sync can take a few minutes.

How do I save an idea from Safari or another app?

Tap the Share button, then choose Gift Ledger. If you don’t see it, scroll the app row to the end, tap More and turn Gift Ledger on. The idea waits in your Inbox on the Ideas tab until you file it under a person.

How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget and search for Gift Ledger. There’s a “Next occasion” widget and a “Thank-yous” widget, including Lock Screen sizes.

I turned off Contacts or notifications. How do I turn them back on?

Open the Settings app › Apps › Gift Ledger. Gift Ledger works fully without either. You can add people and dates by hand.

How do I remove the ads?

Tap the gear on the Upcoming tab, then “Remove ads”. It’s a one-time purchase that removes the ad bar for good on every device signed in to your Apple Account. Already bought it? Tap “Restore purchase”.

Can I back up or export my data?

Yes. Tap the gear on the Upcoming tab, then “Save a full backup to Files” for a complete copy you can restore on any device, or “Export ledger as spreadsheet (CSV)” for a spreadsheet of every gift.

How do I remove the sample ledger?

Tap the gear on the Upcoming tab and choose Remove sample ledger. Only the sample entries are removed; anything you added stays.
